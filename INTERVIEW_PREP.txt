INTERVIEW QUICK PREP

Project: Sales Data Analysis & Reporting Dashboard

1. What was the goal?
To analyze sales data and turn raw records into useful business KPIs and trends.

2. What tools did you use?
SQL for querying and aggregation, and Excel for reporting, KPIs and charts.

3. What cleaning did you perform?
Handled missing values, trimmed inconsistent text values, checked duplicates and recalculated revenue consistently.

4. What KPIs did you calculate?
Total revenue, total units, number of orders and average order value.

5. What business analysis did you perform?
Product, region, sales-channel and monthly revenue analysis.

6. What did you learn?
How to move from raw data to a clean dataset, analytical queries, business metrics and a report/dashboard.

Important:
The dataset is synthetic and was created for portfolio/interview practice.
